# Horizon seven: saved, unrun future work

Publication scope: this is a curated research note. Large search decks,
solver logs, and unrun adapters named in the historical text remain outside
this compact package. Included finite receipts and the Lean theorem sources
are distinguished in [the closeout guide](../README.md).


Status at the 16 October 2026 closeout: **source prepared only**. No focused
test, horizon-seven query, ordinary-card export, or independent acceptance
has run. No launch manifest was issued. The closeout instruction stopped
this proposed extension before execution; the source is retained for a
possible future resumption.

The generator derives separate horizon-seven engine and placement-cache
files from the pinned, tested horizon-six sources. The adapter retains the
complete original P53 11+11 ownership on the physical 10 by 10 board and
adds the same always-available outside-reply class. It rejects P52, whose
complete conditional defense was independently accepted. It does not
select either continued endpoint, which also has an accepted defense.

Six focused controls were written but have not been run. They are intended
to cover the generated-source delta, horizons one through six, a tiny
independent oracle, ordinary-card validity, transactional interruption,
cache invariants, and exact P53 state binding. Their presence is not
evidence that the extension works. There is no verified minimal
seven-move fixture or horizon-seven mathematical result.

At the resulting critical interface, B42 is a complete defensive reply: an explicit 37-pair perfect matching follows. The repair survives arbitrary interleavings of nine specified exterior pairs.

I then give an exact finite reduction that retains the crossing-cell ownership, rather than replacing the remaining problem by an unjustifiably stronger guard game.
